# JobFlow AI — UX & Feature Audit (2026-11-27)

**Scope:** layout and usability on small, medium and large screens, plus missing features. Security, AI truthfulness and billing are already covered in `docs/QA_REPORT_2026-11-25.md` and `docs/V2_ROADMAP.md`, so they are not repeated here. Items that overlap the roadmap are marked **(↔ roadmap x.y)**.

**How it was tested:**
- Local dev server (`next dev --webpack`), signed in as `dev-master-tester`.
- Viewports: 375×812 (phone), 768×1024 (tablet), 1024×700 (small laptop), 1440×900 (desktop).
- At each size, a DOM script measured horizontal overflow, controls under 32 px, text-size distribution, the number of controls in view, and sticky/fixed layers. Screenshots were used to check.
- **Pass 1 (empty state):** `/api/profile` was stubbed in the browser with a fictional profile, so nothing was written and no generation ran.
- **Pass 2 (full run, section 7):**
  - A fictional profile ("Alex Sample") was saved to the `dev-master-tester` account.
  - One application was generated end to end for a made-up job ad (Northwind Mobility, Frontend Engineer).
  - Every AI tool was then run once: outreach, interview prep, cover-letter paragraph polish, section regenerate, bullet polish and project polish.
  - Tokens used: 41 (100 → 59), which matches the price list.

---

## 1. Summary

The app does a lot, and nearly all of it is visible at the same time. The main problem is **density, not missing features**:
- The Tailor page alone has **119 buttons, 6 overlays and 124 `useState` hooks** in one 8,962-line file.
- **120 uses of `text-[10px]`/`[9px]`/`[8px]`**.
- **0 `aria-label`s**.

On a phone, the input the user actually needs (the job description) sits below about 15 optional settings. On a tablet, one bug traps the user in Preview mode. On a desktop, the preview toolbar repeats controls that are already in the left panel.

The fix is mostly **subtraction and progressive disclosure**:
- one primary action per screen;
- advanced settings collapsed by default;
- each control in exactly one place.

### Measured numbers

| Page @ viewport | Controls on page | Controls in first screen | Controls < 32 px | Text nodes < 12 px |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dashboard @ 375 | 18 | 8 | 7 | 19 of 58 |
| Profile @ 375 | 28 | 18 | 3 | 8 of 47 |
| Tailor, Generation tab @ 375 | 38 | 25 | 21 | 24 of 57 |
| Tailor, Customization tab @ 375 | 57 | 21 | **49** | 24 of 44 |
| Tailor @ 1440 (empty, before generating) | 65 | 47 | **63** | 23 of 52 |

Touch guidelines (Apple and Google) recommend targets of at least 44×44 px or 48×48 dp. Most of the Tailor controls are 24–30 px tall.

## 2. Bugs found during the audit (fix first)

### B1. Tablet (640–1023 px): the "Edit & Strategy / Document Preview" switch is covered by the preview toolbar 🔴
- **Where:** `src/app/tailor/page.tsx:5692` (switch bar `sticky top-[49px] sm:top-[65px] z-30`) and `:6553` (preview toolbar `sticky top-[49px] sm:top-0 z-40`).
- **Verified at 768 px:** clicking the middle of "Edit & Strategy" hits the toolbar's **"Cover Letter"** tab instead. Once a tablet user switches to Preview, they can't get back to Edit without reloading.
- **Root cause:** the header height is hard-coded as `49px`/`65px`, but it really measures **67 px** on phone and **75 px** from `sm` up. The toolbar's `sm:top-0` together with a higher z-index lets it slide over the switch bar.
- **Fix:**
  - Expose the header height as a CSS variable (e.g. `--app-header-h`, set from a ResizeObserver or a fixed `h-16`) and use `top-[var(--app-header-h)]` everywhere.
  - Make the switch bar's z-index higher than the toolbar's.

### B2. Phone: the mobile bottom-sheet handle overlaps the bottom nav 🟠
In Preview mode at 375 px, the bottom-sheet peek bar is fixed at y 706–758 and the bottom nav at y 731–812, a 27 px overlap. The nav's "Tailor AI" hero button (`-top-2`, raised) sits on top of the sheet handle. Either offset the sheet by the measured nav height, or hide the global bottom nav on `/tailor` while in Preview.

### B3. Magic-number heights throughout the Tailor page 🟠
- `h-[calc(100vh-125px)]`, `min-h-[calc(100vh-73px)]` and `max-h-[calc(100vh-73px)]` assume a 73 px header plus a 52 px switch bar. Neither is right at any measured size.
- At 1440 px the document is 902 px tall in a 900 px viewport, so there is a 2 px page scroll on top of the inner pane scrolls (double scrollbars).
- `100vh` also ignores the mobile browser toolbar.
- **Fix:** use `100dvh` and a flex column (`header / switch / flex-1 min-h-0 overflow-auto`) instead of calc'd heights.

### B4. Empty-state copy says "on the left" on mobile 🟡
"Provide the job details **on the left**…" (Document Preview empty state) is shown on phone and tablet, where there is no left panel. Use "in the Edit tab" below `lg`.

### B5. Two "Save Vault" buttons visible at once on mobile Profile 🟡
At 375 px the header "Save Vault" (full width) and the floating "Save Vault" pill (`aria-label="Floating Mobile Save"`) are both on screen in the first viewport. Show only the floating one on mobile, and only when `isDirty`.

## 3. Cross-cutting issues

### 3.1 Too much chrome on small screens
Tailor → Preview at 375 px stacks **six** sticky/fixed layers:

| Layer | Height |
|---|---|
| App header | 67 px |
| Edit/Preview switch | 53 px |
| Document tabs (Resume / Cover Letter / Outreach / Interview Prep) | ~56 px |
| Zoom HUD | 36 px |
| Bottom-sheet peek | 52 px |
| Bottom nav | 81 px |

That leaves **~437 px of 812 (54 %)** for the document.

Recommendations:
- **Hide the global bottom nav on `/tailor`** (it is a focused workspace) and show a back arrow in the header instead.
- Merge the Edit/Preview switch and the document tabs into **one** segmented row: `Edit · CV · Letter · More ▾`.
- Collapse the header on scroll-down and reveal it on scroll-up.

### 3.2 Tiny type and tiny targets everywhere
- `text-[10px]` is used for interactive labels (format selector, spacing presets, Compare, Photo, ATS highlight, kanban counts).
- Small labels are uppercase with wide tracking, which reads even smaller.
- **Rule of thumb:**
  - 12 px minimum for any text.
  - 14 px for anything you tap.
  - 44 px minimum touch height below `lg` (`min-h-11`).
- Add these to a shared `Button` / `Segmented` / `Chip` component so the sizes are enforced once rather than per class string.

### 3.3 The same control appears in more than one place
| Control | Locations |
|---|---|
| CV format (Visual / Strict ATS / Bullet Matrix) | Left panel → Generation → "CV Format & Prompting Style" (`:5909`) **and** the preview toolbar (`:6646`) |
| Spacing presets (Default / Compact / Ultra-Tight) | Left panel → Customization (`:6497`) **and** the preview toolbar (`:6681`) **and** the Fine-Tune popover (`:6860`) |
| Fine spacing sliders | Customization tab **and** the Fine-Tune popover |
| Section order / visibility | Customization → Document Sections **and** the "Section Layout Order" dropdown (`:6711`) **and** the mobile bottom sheet |

Pick one home for each:
- **Generation settings** (what the AI writes) go in the left panel.
- **Presentation settings** (how it looks) go in a single "Design" panel next to the preview: a popover on desktop, the bottom sheet on mobile.

### 3.4 Jargon and marketing copy inside the tool
Labels such as "Hyper-aligned outputs", "Document Preview Engine", "Command Center", "Master Professional Vault", "Bootstrap with AI Profile Auto-Importer", "AI Description Nudging", "Tactical Pivot / Aggressive Bridging" and "Bullet Matrix" make the user decode the product before they can use it. Suggested plain labels:

| Current | Suggested |
|---|---|
| Master Professional Vault | Your profile |
| AI Tailoring Workspace | Tailor a CV |
| Bootstrap with AI Profile Auto-Importer | Import from CV, LinkedIn or GitHub |
| Tactical Pivot / Aggressive Bridging | Stay close to my profile / Stretch to fit the job (with a one-line explainer) |
| AI Description Nudging | Adapt my descriptions to the job |
| Document Preview Engine (empty state) | Your tailored CV will appear here |

Emoji inside button labels (📋 Kanban Board, ⚖️ Compare, 📷 Photo) clash with the lucide icons right next to them. Use one or the other.

### 3.5 Accessibility
- **0 `aria-label`s** on the Dashboard and Tailor pages, and 1 on Profile. Icon-only buttons (zoom ±, persona duplicate/rename, kanban ← / →, theme toggle) have no accessible name.
- Panel tabs (Generation / ATS & Insights / Customization; Resume / Cover Letter / …) are plain buttons with no `role="tab"`, `aria-selected` or arrow-key navigation.
- Status is shown by colour alone in the kanban column headers and the ATS highlight.
- Six modals (`fixed inset-0 z-[9999]`) are hand-rolled, with no focus trap, no Esc handling and no `aria-modal`. A shared `<Dialog>` would fix all of them at once.
- Some `animate-pulse` / `animate-ping` effects run constantly (the "Tailor Workspace" header button and the active nav dot) and ignore `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### 3.6 Feedback and confirmations
- `window.confirm` is still used for the unsaved-changes guard on Profile, while the rest of the app uses `AlertModalContext`. Use one pattern.
- Token cost is shown on the Generate button ("20 tokens"), which is good. Section regeneration, bullet polish, outreach and interview prep should show their cost the same way before the click.

### 4.3 Tailor workspace (`src/app/tailor/page.tsx`)
This page is where the feeling of being overwhelmed comes from.

**Before generation (Generation tab), current order on mobile:**
1. Title and marketing blurb
2. Token balance card
3. Tabs: Generation / ATS & Insights / Customization
4. Drafts list
5. Persona selector
6. Company name, Role name
7. CV language, Cover-letter language
8. Tailoring customizations: format (3-way), tone, length target, letter length, AI Description Nudging toggle …
9. Match strategy (2-way)
10. Job intake method (Paste / URL / PDF)
11. **Job description** ← the one required input
12. Additional details / overrides (salary, notice, signing location, focus notes, theme)
13. Missing-assets warning
14. **Generate** button

**Recommended order (a three-step wizard on mobile, one column on desktop):**
1. **Job.** A single smart field: paste text **or** a URL (auto-detected) **or** drop a PDF. Company and role are auto-filled from the posting and remain editable.
2. **Options**, collapsed under a summary chip: `EN · Visual · 1 page · Stay close to profile ▾`. Advanced options (tone, nudging, overrides, salary, notice, theme) go behind "More options".
3. **Generate (20 tokens)** as a sticky footer button, always visible.

| # | Issue | Recommendation |

On a phone the user has to zoom or pinch, and inline editing at that scale is impractical. | On phone, offer a **reflowed "reading view"** of the CV (same content, mobile typography) for review and editing, with "Preview as A4" as a secondary toggle. ## 5. Missing features and improvements

Grouped by value; ★ marks the most valuable items.

### Workflow
- ★ **Autosave of tailor inputs.** The job description, overrides and options are lost when leaving the page; there is no local persistence in `tailor/page.tsx`. Persist them per draft. (↔ roadmap 4.1)
- ★ **One "application" object end to end:** job → tailored CV and letter → export → tracker card → follow-ups. The Dashboard and Tailor currently feel like separate tools. Opening a tracker card should open its tailored documents, and the reverse.
- ★ **Export menu in one place** (PDF / DOCX / plain-text ATS / copy), with a filename preset (`Lastname_Company_CV.pdf`).
- **Version history per application.** Keep the previous generations and restore one, instead of only undo/redo.
- **Follow-up reminders** (the kanban "aging pulse" exists): add due dates and an optional email or browser notification.
- **Duplicate application.** Start a new tailoring from an existing one (same role type, different company).

### Onboarding
- ★ **First-run guided path:** import a CV → check the profile → tailor a sample job, with a sample job description so the first try costs nothing (or is cached/free).
- **Profile completeness meter** (P4).
- **Explain the strategies** with a before/after example instead of names only.

### Mobile
- **Paste a job URL from the share sheet.** Make the app a PWA with a Web Share Target so "Share → JobFlow" from LinkedIn or a browser opens Tailor with the URL filled in.
- **Reading view for the CV** (T5).
- **Bottom-sheet Design panel** as the only place for visual settings on mobile.

### Insight
- **Single match score** with a breakdown (↔ roadmap 3.3), shown next to the preview header rather than in a separate tab.
- **Keyword chips** ("covered / missing"), each tappable to show where in the CV it appears.

### Settings and trust
- **Settings page** (currently scattered across the user menu): default language, default CV format, default strategy, photo on/off by region, theme, delete account/data (↔ roadmap 7.4).
- **Token history** (what each token was spent on), next to the balance.

### 7.3 Scoring and insight panel inconsistencies

- **Two scores disagree:** "71 % profile fit" in one place and "Keywords in CV 7/11 (64 %)" in another. (↔ roadmap 3.3)
- **The "Matching keywords" chips list "React" twice and include "Cypress",** while the text right above says "Not in the CV: … Cypress". The Dashboard drawer repeats the same duplicate list.
- **"Not in the CV" names 4 keywords but "Missing / skill gaps" lists 3** (Cypress missing). The counts don't line up.
- The **"ADD ALL MISSING KEYWORDS (3)"** and **"Add Skill"** buttons invite users to add skills they don't have (GraphQL, Next.js), which goes against the bridging/truthfulness design.
- **The job's requirement was softened** when it was stored: the ad says "Experience with GraphQL (Apollo Client)", but the saved core requirements say "Experience *or willingness* to work with GraphQL". This flatters the match.

### 7.4 Bridged-claims review: noisy and incomplete

The panel showed 4 claims to review:

| Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|
| "WCAG/accessibility moved to the A11y Checker CLI project" | Fair |
| "JavaScript added to A11y Checker CLI tech list" — *listed in your skills* | **False positive.** The skill is in the profile, and a Node.js CLI is written in JavaScript. |
| "Cypress — bridged from Jest" (cover letter) | Correct |
| "Figure: 62,000 — not in your profile" | **False positive.** The user typed it into the Salary field on the same form. |

So **half the flags were noise**, while real inflations were **not flagged**:
- "basic accessibility fixes (WCAG AA colour contrast, alt text)" became "ensuring **strict adherence to WCAG AA** accessibility standards".
- "shipment-tracking dashboard" became "**real-time** logistics monitoring". "Real-time" appears nowhere in the profile, and it then spread into the cover letter, outreach, the interview pitch and all three bullet-polish variants.
- The cover letter says "**REST services**" and "three **engineering** teams" (the profile says *product* teams).

Good news: after the cover-letter paragraph was replaced, the Cypress claim disappeared from the list (4 → 3), so claims are re-checked after edits.

**Recommendation:**
- Check figures against the overrides as well as the vault.
- Don't flag a skill the profile already has.
- Add a check for new qualifiers such as "real-time", "strict", "architected" and "led".

### 7.5 Downstream AI tools inherit and amplify errors (↔ QA report, "contaminates downstream")

- **Interview prep, elevator pitch:** "established a multi-team Storybook design system with Jest testing **that reduced form-related support tickets by roughly a third**". The 33 % came from the jQuery→React migration, not Storybook. The talking points are labelled "**FROM YOUR REAL EXPERIENCE**".
- **Cover-letter polish:** 2 of the 3 variants repeat the same misattribution.
- **Project polish,** "Impact & Metrics" variant: "seamless offline state **synchronization** and **zero-latency** client-side data management". Both are invented; the profile only says offline-first with IndexedDB.
- **Section-regen presets** are not job-aware: "Highlight cloud & modern devops tech" is offered for a frontend role. "More metric-focused & STAR formula" pushes toward invented metrics.
- **Polish variant labels** include "Formal Corporate (**DIN 5008**)" on an English letter. DIN 5008 is a German letter standard.

**Recommendation:** feed the master profile, not the tailored CV, into outreach, interview prep and all polish prompts, and run the bridged-claims check on their output too.

### 7.6 Outreach usability

- The **LinkedIn connection note is 560 characters**. LinkedIn limits connection notes to 300 (200 on free accounts), so it can't be pasted as is. Add a live character counter and generate to the limit.
- The editable message boxes have a fixed height: the email body box shows 162 px of 356 px of content. Make them grow with their content.
- `[Name]` / `[Hiring Manager Name]` placeholders stay in the text even though there is an optional "Contact person" field. Highlight unfilled placeholders, and block "Copy" until they are filled (or warn).

### 7.7 What worked well

- **Everything persisted:** reopening the application from the Dashboard ("Open in Tailoring Workspace") restored the CV edits, the polished paragraph, outreach and interview prep. "Draft · saved" is shown in the toolbar.
- **Token accounting is correct** (41 charged, 41 expected), and costs are shown on every button.
- **The Dashboard card and drawer** are clear: status stepper, salary/notice, extracted tech stack, "Open in Tailoring Workspace", follow-up draft and calendar.
- **Interview-prep "Pitfalls & Traps"** tells the user directly not to claim GraphQL/Cypress experience. That's a good honesty nudge; it should be visible next to the CV as well.
- **The Export menu** has the three formats you'd expect (PDF, DOCX, ATS plain text). Exports were not run in this pass.

- **R1/R2**, collapse the preview toolbar into one row. This is the single biggest UX win.
- **R3**, ask before charging, everywhere.
- **R4/R5**, toasts instead of success modals, plus Esc to close.
- The two false-positive rules in 7.4.

Add to the **roadmap's phase 2 (truthfulness)**: 7.4 (missing qualifier checks) and 7.5 (feed the master profile into downstream tools).
