# CV & Resume Tailoring Guidelines

A set of professional guidelines for generating optimized, high-impact CVs and cover letters for job applications.

---

## 💼 1. Work History & Experience
*   **Internal Promotions:** Treat each role/promotion within the same company as a separate job entry to demonstrate professional growth and organizational loyalty.
*   **Internships:** Always append `(Internship)` (or `(Praktikum)` in German) next to the job title.
*   **Relevancy Limits:** Limit detailed work history to the **last four companies** to keep the CV clean. Quantify the total years of professional experience in the summary/statement instead.

## 🎯 2. Skills & Hobbies
*   **Soft Skills:** Do not list soft skills standalone. Integrate them directly into the professional summary and achievement bullets using the **"Show, Don't Tell"** approach (e.g., *"Strong communication skills developed through customer-facing roles..."*).
*   **Hobbies:** Omit generic hobbies (e.g., reading, traveling). Only include hobbies if they directly demonstrate workplace traits (e.g., leadership, teamwork) or are highly relevant to the role.

## 🚀 3. Bullet Point Optimization (Software Developers)
Transform weak descriptions into high-impact, metrics-driven outcomes:
*   *Weak:* "responsible for coding new features"
*   *Average:* "coded new features for the online shop"
*   *Good:* "coded new features for the online shop and increased page-speed by 60%"
*   *Great:* "increased the conversion rate of the online shop by 25% by modernizing the tech stack and increasing page speed by 60%"

## 📝 4. Technicalities
*   **References:** Never use the phrase "References available upon request". Keep references off the CV; manage them in a separate document.
*   **Pronouns:** Gender pronouns are optional. If included, place them directly under the signature block as: `"Pronouns: [your pronouns]"`.

## 🇩🇪 5. German Market & Job Hunting Tactics
*   **Master CV:** Maintain a comprehensive Master CV as a private database. Only share tailored versions.
*   **Direct Application:** Avoid using LinkedIn "Easy Apply" if applying to German roles from abroad. Always apply directly on the company's official website to bypass automatic resident filters.
*   **August Slowdown:** Expect delays in hiring decisions and communications during August due to summer vacations.
*   **Job Archiving:** Always save job descriptions locally, as postings are frequently removed by the interview stage.
